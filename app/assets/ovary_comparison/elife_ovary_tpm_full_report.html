Errors are reanalysis minus published values. The diagonal bands are forced by the coordinates: when published TPM is zero, error = +2 × average log-expression; when reanalysis TPM is zero, error = −2 × average log-expression. Of the 17,007 pairs with absolute error >2, 63.5% contain an exact zero. Published TPM was ≥10 while reanalysis TPM was zero in 4,073 pairs; the reverse occurred in 163. Density plots clip only the outer 0.1% for readable axes; summary metrics use the full distribution.

Green denotes published 0 → reanalysis nonzero; red denotes published nonzero → reanalysis 0. An exact zero can mean no compatible fragments were assigned under that quantification model; it is not a universal biological absence threshold. Threshold bars therefore ask how large the value is on the nonzero side. There are 6,924 exact published 0 → reanalysis nonzero pairs and 54,162 published nonzero → reanalysis 0 pairs. At a nonzero-side threshold of 1 TPM these fall to 1,477 and 19,638; at 10 TPM, 163 and 4,073.
